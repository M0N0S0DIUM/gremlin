Gremlin Sprite Sheet Spec
========================
For the Gremlin desktop character (Wayland layer-shell window).
https://github.com/M0N0S0DIUM/gremlin

TECHNICAL
---------
Format:       PNG, RGBA (transparent background)
Frame size:   24×24 pixels each
Sheet size:   672×24 pixels (28 frames × 24px, single horizontal strip)
Display size: 48×48 (rendered at 2x scale, nearest-neighbor — stays crisp)
Palette:      Your choice. Current placeholder uses #00ff41 (green phosphor)
              on transparent. CRT/terminal aesthetic preferred — not cartoon.

Character:    Small round creature with horns/ears, sits bottom-center of frame.
              Not a cute mascot — a sarcastic senior coworker that lives in
              your terminal. Two dots for eyes is plenty. Mouth optional.

ORDER (left to right on the sprite sheet)
=========================================

─── STATE 0: SLEEPING (4 frames, loops at 4fps) ───
When: Default state. Shown ~90% of the time when Gremlin is idle.
F0: Breathe in  — body slightly expanded, glow brighter
F1: Hold       — peak inhale
F2: Breathe out — body contracted, dimmer
F3: Rest       — minimum size, faintest glow
Feel: Peaceful. Curled up. Eyes closed (two horizontal dashes). Unnoticeable.

─── STATE 1: WAKING UP (3 frames, plays ONCE on summon) ───
When: User presses Super + ' to summon Gremlin. Transitions to AWAKE IDLE.
F0: Stretch    — arms up, body elongated, eyes still closed
F1: Rub eyes   — one arm at face, one eye starting to open
F2: Sit up     — alert posture, both eyes open, ready
Feel: "Ugh, fine, I'm up." Not rushed. Slightly reluctant.

─── STATE 2: AWAKE IDLE (4 frames, loops at 4fps) ───
When: Gremlin is summoned but not actively doing anything. Waiting for input.
F0: Eyes open  — neutral/alert, sitting upright
F1: Half blink — eyes half-closed (vertical slits, not dashes)
F2: Eyes shut  — full blink (horizontal dashes like sleeping, but brief)
F3: Eyes open  — back to neutral, maybe slightly shifted posture
Feel: Attentive but relaxed. The blink cycle makes it feel alive.
      Optional: tiny sway (±1px horizontal offset between cycles).

─── STATE 3: READING (2 frames, loops at 3fps) ───
When: Gremlin is running read_file, git_status, git_diff, or similar tools.
F0: Eyes left  — pupils/eyes shifted left, tiny glasses on
F1: Eyes right — pupils/eyes shifted right (scanning text)
Feel: Focused. Glasses are important visual cue — user should immediately
      know Gremlin is "reading" something.

─── STATE 4: THINKING (3 frames, loops at 3fps) ───
When: LLM inference in progress. Gremlin is waiting for Ollama to respond.
F0: Look up    — eyes aimed upward, slight lean back
F1: Look down  — eyes aimed downward, hand near chin area
F2: Chin rub   — hand on chin, eyes narrowed (like "hmm...")
Feel: Pondering. This is the user's signal that Gremlin is working,
      not frozen. Should feel like genuine thought, not a loading spinner.

─── STATE 5: BUILDING (3 frames, loops at 2fps) ───
When: cargo_build or cargo_test is running.
F0: Sip coffee — tiny cup at mouth area, eyes relaxed
F1: Watch      — cup lowered, eyes tracking to the side (watching progress)
F2: Coffee rest— cup resting on lap/knee, patient expression
Feel: Patient. This is the "watching a long compile" mood. The coffee cup
      is the key visual — user should know a build is happening.

─── STATE 6: TYPING (3 frames, loops at 6fps — fast!) ───
When: Gremlin is launching Hermes or writing a file.
F0: Press left  — left arm down at keyboard position, body hunched
F1: Press right — right arm down, left arm up
F2: Press both  — both arms down, slightly different posture
Feel: Fast, busy, working. Higher framerate (6fps) sells the typing effect.
      This is the most energetic state. Shows Gremlin is actively doing work.

─── STATE 7: ERROR (2 frames, loops at 2fps) ───
When: A tool failed, Hermes returned an error, or something went wrong.
F0: Tilt left  — head tilted ~15° left, "?" visible nearby, eyes open
F1: Tilt right — head tilted ~15° right, still confused
Feel: Concerned but not panicked. "You seeing this?" energy.
      The question mark is important — it's Gremlin's way of saying
      "something's not right" without speaking.

─── AMBIENT: YAWN (2 frames) ───
Plays randomly, very rarely, when in AWAKE IDLE state.
F0: Pre-yawn  — mouth starting to open, eyes half-closed
F1: Full yawn — mouth wide, eyes fully shut, maybe arms stretch up

─── AMBIENT: SCRATCH EAR (2 frames) ───
Plays randomly, very rarely, when in AWAKE IDLE state.
F0: Arm up    — one arm raised toward ear/head
F1: Scratch   — arm at ear, slight head tilt toward the scratch

─── AMBIENT: CHECK WATCH (2 frames) ───
Plays randomly, very rarely, when in AWAKE IDLE state.
F0: Look down — eyes aimed at wrist area, arm raised slightly
F1: Check     — tiny watch face visible, impatient expression
Feel: "How long has this build been running?" Very occasional.

LAYOUT SUMMARY
==============
Frame 0-3:    Sleeping (4)
Frame 4-6:    Waking Up (3)
Frame 7-10:   Awake Idle (4)
Frame 11-12:  Reading (2)
Frame 13-15:  Thinking (3)
Frame 16-18:  Building (3)
Frame 19-21:  Typing (3)
Frame 22-23:  Error (2)
Frame 24-25:  Yawn (2)
Frame 26-27:  Scratch Ear (2)
Frame 28-29:  (reserved — Check Watch or future)

EMOTIONAL RANGE
===============
Gremlin should never look:
- Cute/moe/kawaii — it's a coworker, not a pet
- Angry/aggressive — it's sarcastic, not hostile
- Sad/pathetic — it's competent, not pitiful
- Hyperactive — it's understated, not a cartoon

Gremlin should feel like:
- The senior dev who's seen everything
- Dry, understated reactions
- Occasional genuine approval (tiny thumbs up? rare raised eyebrow?)
- "I'm not mad, I'm just disappointed" energy when things break

WHAT TO IGNORE
==============
- Speech bubbles (rendered by the Wayland compositor)
- Background (transparent — compositor handles the dark CRT background)
- Scanlines (overlay applied at render time)
- Smooth animations between states (programmatic tweens, not sprite frames)
- The summon/dismiss animation (scale transform, not sprites)
- Progress bars, coffee steam, or other effects (programmatic)
